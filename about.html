About Government Junior College, Narsapur

Government Junior College, Narsapur, located in Medak District, Telangana, is a reputed institution dedicated to providing quality intermediate education to students from diverse backgrounds.

The institution offers MPC, BiPC, CEC, HEC, and ACE streams and focuses on academic excellence, discipline, and holistic development.

Vision

To provide quality education that inspires academic excellence, critical thinking, and responsible citizenship.

Mission

  • Provide accessible and affordable education.
  • Promote excellence in teaching and learning.
  • Encourage discipline and integrity.
  • Prepare students for higher education and careers.
  • Foster overall student development.
